Sleep Left

0.1.0 · Rootless · Free · MIT

Live hours/minutes/seconds in ordinary Clock alarm subtitles, including disabled alarms. Native labels and controls stay in place; snooze and scheduling stay unchanged.

Disabled countdowns preview the configured next occurrence. They do not enable an alarm. Sleep/Wake Up is excluded. No standalone app or settings bundle.

Add repository to SileoSourceSupport

Install and use

  1. Add https://0chium.github.io/ to Sileo and refresh sources.
  2. Install Sleep Left.
  3. Fully close and reopen Clock, then open Alarms.

The countdown appears automatically. No separate app, settings pane or extra configuration. AlarmTime and Choicy are not required.

Requires a compatible conventional rootless jailbreak and Substrate-compatible loader. See the tested configurations below.

Owner-tested configurations

The owner reports initial functional passes for 0.1.0~alpha1 on iPhone 14 Pro — iOS 17.0 (21A329) and iPhone 11 Pro Max — iOS 18.3.2 (22D82), without another alarm-countdown tweak active. Checks covered enabled/disabled countdowns, custom labels, editing/toggling, scrolling without duplicate or mismatched text, and catch-up after returning to Clock. These are owner reports, not comprehensive compatibility, accessibility, calendar-edge-case or performance certification. The final 0.1.0 package has not itself received device testing.

Other countdown tweaks

If you already use another alarm-countdown tweak, disable or remove it before using Sleep Left so only one modifies the alarm rows.

Limitations

Sleep/Wake Up is excluded. No blanket iOS 17/18 or iOS 26 support. Other configurations and RootHide conversion are unverified. Recurrence/DST and snooze/time-zone edge cases are not comprehensively tested. VoiceOver custom content and large text remain unverified by owner testing. No measured battery, CPU or reliability claims. Countdown units are English.

Implementation and what we learned

Apple supplies the next occurrence. We append to the native subtitle and preserve native alarm controls. One timer serves the visible active alarm list. Text is regenerated from the original subtitle to avoid duplicate suffixes. VoiceOver custom content is an implementation choice, currently unverified by owner testing. These choices do not imply knowledge of AlarmTime internals or superiority over it.
