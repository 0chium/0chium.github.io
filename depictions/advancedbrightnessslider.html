AdvancedBrightnessSlider Continued

1.2.2-1 · Rootless · Free · GPL-3.0

An independently maintained compatibility fork of Jonas Schiefner's AdvancedBrightnessSlider, maintained by 0chium.

Extra dimming through Apple's existing Control Center brightness slider. Above your threshold, adjust ordinary brightness. Below it, hold ordinary brightness at minimum and progressively adjust native Reduce White Point.

Add repository to SileoSourceSupport

Or add https://0chium.github.io/ manually. This is the existing unsigned flat APT repository; the link adds the source and does not install a package.

Replacement and preferences

Sileo should remove com.jschiefner.advancedbrightnessslider when installing this package. Review that transaction; do not force co-installation. The existing preference storage and injection paths are retained.

The owner reports successful candidate migration with settings preserved on the 18.3.2 target. The final source/support-link rebuild has not been installed; it changes presentation and attribution, not brightness behavior or exact-build guards.

Dependencies: mobilesubstrate ≥ 0.9.5000 and Cephei (ws.hbang.common) ≥ 1.17. Package architecture: iphoneos-arm64; binaries contain arm64 and arm64e slices.

Known limits

  • Auto-Brightness suppression retains the existing implementation; its native-service effectiveness is not independently verified.
  • Accessibility White Point changes may affect the screen without repositioning Control Center.
  • Controlled disablement conditionally restores borrowed settings. Crash, SpringBoard restart or removal cannot guarantee cleanup; system settings may remain changed.
  • The legacy iOS 16 path is retained but this rebuilt release is not runtime validated there. Modern alternative Control Centers are not claimed as supported.
