Lantern

Version 0.1.3-1 · Free

A warmer light. Right in Control Center.

Tap Lantern for warm light. Press and hold to adjust brightness with Apple's native intensity slider. Keep the stock flashlight for white light.

No Settings pane or preferences to configure.

Compatibility & known issues

Verified working: iPhone SE (2nd generation), iOS 18.2 (22C152), Dopamine 3.0.10 rootless. Physical white/warm/off behavior was verified on device.

Owner-verified after activation: iPhone 11 Pro Max (iPhone12,5), iOS 18.3.2 (22D82), Dopamine 3 rootless, ElleKit 1.2, CCSupport installed. Physical warm/OFF, native white ON/OFF, and return to warm passed after the documented activation procedure. Its initial white-only symptom resolved; the precise internal cause remains unproven.

The SE 2 reference used CCSupport 1.3.13-3~ios18fix1; the D10 CCSupport version was not recorded. Requires compatible CCSupport and mobilesubstrate. Other configurations are unverified.

  • Owner-observed: iPhone 14 Pro, iOS 17.0 (21A329), Dopamine rootless: selecting Lantern crashes SpringBoard during Control Center initialization and enters Safe Mode. The cause is unknown. Warm-light capability on iPhone 14 Pro is unverified.
  • Tester-reported, not independently verified: iPhone 11 Pro, iOS 18.1.1, converted RootHide installation: the control appears, but the light reportedly remains white. Conversion does not establish compatibility.
  • Other reports, not independently verified: Safe Mode on iOS 16.5.1 and functionality on other iOS 18 configurations. These do not establish broader support; reported failure causes remain unknown.

If a crash or Safe Mode occurs, stop testing and preserve existing reports. Do not reproduce a crash to collect logs.

Known limitations on the tested configuration
  • Switching between Lantern and Apple's flashlight at the same brightness may require a second interaction to update the physical light.
  • Apple's expanded flashlight active indicator may appear while Lantern operates.

Install and use

Install Lantern from 0chium's Repo, complete activation, then add Lantern using Control Center's editing controls. Requires compatible CCSupport.

Activation on tested Dopamine rootless configurations

Turn the flashlight off and close Camera before installation, updates or removal. The package has no automatic activation scripts. Restart cameracaptured and reload SpringBoard using the documented activation procedure.

A respring alone is not the verified complete procedure. RootHide activation and recovery remain unverified.

What's new in 0.1.3-1

Version-only metadata update from 0.1.2-1. All installed binaries and resources are byte-identical. No functional code, LED mapping, injection, dependency or activation changes were made.

Physical verification used 0.1.2-1 on the configurations above. Testing of the newly versioned package upgrade is pending. The same-intensity handoff limitation remains.
