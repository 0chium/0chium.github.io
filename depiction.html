Lantern

Version 0.1.2-1 · Free

Warm light for Control Center.

Tap Lantern for warm/yellow light. Press and hold to adjust brightness with Apple's native intensity slider. The stock flashlight remains available for white light.

Check compatibility and known issues before installing.

Compatibility & known issues

Verified working: iPhone SE (2nd generation), iOS 18.2 (22C152), Dopamine 3.0.10 rootless. Physical white/warm/off behavior was verified on device.

Tested with CCSupport 1.3.13-3~ios18fix1. Requires compatible CCSupport and mobilesubstrate. Other configurations are unverified.

  • Owner-observed: iPhone 14 Pro, iOS 17.0 (21A329), Dopamine rootless: selecting Lantern crashes SpringBoard during Control Center initialization and enters Safe Mode. The cause is unknown. Warm-light capability on iPhone 14 Pro is unverified.
  • Tester-reported, not independently verified: iPhone 11 Pro, iOS 18.1.1, converted RootHide installation: the control appears, but the light reportedly remains white. Conversion does not establish compatibility.
  • Other reports, not independently verified: Safe Mode on iOS 16.5.1 and functionality on other iOS 18 configurations. These do not establish broader support; reported failure causes remain unknown.

If a crash or Safe Mode occurs, stop testing and preserve existing reports. Do not reproduce a crash to collect logs.

Known limitations on the tested configuration
  • Switching between Lantern and Apple's flashlight at the same brightness may require a second interaction to update the physical light.
  • Apple's expanded flashlight active indicator may appear while Lantern operates.

Installation

Turn the flashlight off and close Camera before installing, updating or removing Lantern. Use your package manager for those operations.

The package has no automatic activation scripts. On the verified Dopamine rootless environment, restart cameracaptured and reload SpringBoard using the documented activation procedure. A respring alone is not the verified complete procedure. RootHide activation and recovery remain unverified.

Unsigned repository. Sileo source refresh and the Lantern listing were verified on the reference device; this does not establish every installation, update or removal scenario.

What's new in 0.1.2-1

This revision adds the required CCSupport dependency to package metadata. Flashlight behavior and installed files are unchanged.

Successful and unsuccessful installations are useful. Report compatibility from observations already made, or report a bug.
